# 0.8.5 — 2026-09-22

## Added

- **`protein_track_ligands` `binding_site`** — pocket residues now carry author numbering (`authAsymId`/`authSeqId`) beside the mmCIF label numbering (`asymId`/`seqId`), and the ligand instance adds its author residue number (`ligandAuthSeqId`). The author chain comes from the entry's per-instance label/author chain pairs; field descriptions and the rendered text name each namespace, including `ligandAsymId` as an author chain ID (#69).

## Fixed

- **`protein_track_ligands` `find_ligand`** — responses now report `totalCount` (the upstream match total) and `candidatesConsidered` (the components pulled into the deposition-frequency ranking), with a `notice` to narrow the query when a broad name's matches exceed that pool; previously the total was discarded and the truncation went undisclosed (#67).
- **`protein_track_ligands` `binding_site`** — ligand instances now page with `start`/`limit`, reporting `totalCount`/`start`/`nextStart`, naming the next `start` in the notice, and giving a past-end `start` its own notice; previously `start` was ignored in this mode and the notice pointed at a `limit` above its 100 cap, so instances past the first 100 were unreachable (#75).
