# 0.8.7 — 2026-10-16

## Changed

- **Annotation records** retain source-provided variant IDs, original/alternative sequences, and the requested protein's separate InterPro locations and fragments, including discontinuity qualifiers; missing changes remain unknown. ([#68](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/68))
- **Structure comparisons** relay optional raw `similarityScore` and fractional `sequenceSimilarity` on both response surfaces, preserving source presence and resumed-job orientation. ([#72](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/72))
- **Runtime and development guidance** use Bun 1.4.2 → 1.4.3 and synchronized framework skills.

## Fixed

- **Annotation paging** accepts independent `features_offset`, `variants_offset`, and `domains_offset`, returns requested-class totals, and gives concrete continuation or exhausted-offset recovery within the existing 200-record page cap. Pages reflect freshly fetched upstream arrays. ([#65](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/65))
- **RCSB sequence searches** normalize whitespace and ASCII lowercase for raw and identifier-resolved sequences, with typed `invalid_sequence` recovery for inputs outside RCSB's alphabet or 10-residue minimum. ([#77](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/77))
