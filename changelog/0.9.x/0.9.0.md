# 0.9.0 — 2026-11-03

## Added

- **`protein-guide` skill** published through `createApp({ skills })` as the resource `skill://protein-guide/SKILL.md`, with the `skill://{+path}` resource template: identifier chains and the gates for reading `protein_*` results.
- **`protein_search_structures` `ligand` filter** keeps entries holding the given chemical component ID as a bound copy ([#95](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/95)).
- **Sequence-hit alignments**: `identity`, `evalue`, `bitscore`, `alignmentLength`, `queryStart`, `queryEnd`, `targetStart`, `targetEnd`, and `targetLength` on `protein_search_structures` and `protein_find_similar` sequence hits, with `queryLength` on the result ([#89](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/89)).
- **Foldseek structure-hit fields**: `target`, `chain`, `title`, `organisms`, `taxId`, `probability`, `identity`, the aligned span, and `queryLength` ([#88](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/88)).
- **`protein_find_similar` `chain` and `queryEntityId`**: `chain` picks the protein entity of `pdb_id` that `by: "sequence"` searches; `queryEntityId` names the entity used ([#84](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/84)).
- **`protein_get_structure` `best_available` fields** `coverage`, `uniprotStart`, and `uniprotEnd`; `pdbId` is set only when the pick is a PDB entry ([#90](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/90)).
- **Isoform accessions** (`P04637-2`) are accepted by `protein_get_structure` under `predicted` and `best_available`, `af://`, and `protein_find_similar`; **isoform entry IDs** (`AF-P04637-2-F1`) by `protein_get_structure` and `af://`, and `best_available` reduces any AlphaFold DB entry ID to its accession ([#91](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/91)).
- **`protein_find_similar` `uniprot` takes a UniProtKB entry name** (`HBA_HUMAN`) under both `by` modes, resolved to its entry's accession with no merge notice.
- **`ignored`** on `protein_get_structure` and `protein_compare_structures` lists the inputs a batch cap left unprocessed ([#93](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/93)).
- **`protein_get_structure` `coords_offset`**, with `coordinatesOffset` and `coordinatesNextOffset` on records, pages one structure's coordinate file ([#94](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/94)).
- **`protein_track_ligands`** `structures_with_ligand` rows carry `title`, `method`, and `hostEntities` ([#92](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/92)).
- **`protein_get_annotations`** reads 27 UniProt feature fields (was 11), adding cross-links, lipidation, topology, DNA binding, chains, mutagenesis, and non-standard residues ([#82](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/82)).

## Changed

- **Breaking: `organism` (string) is now `organisms` (string[])** on `protein_get_structure` records and polymer entities, `protein_search_structures` hits, `protein_find_similar` hits, and `pdb://` polymer entities. It lists every distinct source organism in entity-number order; a sequence hit lists those of the matched entity ([#85](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/85)).
- **Breaking: `protein_find_similar` structure hits omit `id`** when the Foldseek target names no PDB entry or UniProt accession (`bfvd_v3`, `mgnify_esm30`, `gmgcl_id`, `bfmd`); read `target` and `database` ([#87](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/87)).
- **Breaking: an isoform accession in a `source: "experimental"` batch** of `protein_get_structure` fails the whole call as `mixed_id_types`, as a canonical accession already did ([#91](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/91)).
- **Breaking: `protein_find_similar` `by: "structure"`** rejects a query coordinate file longer than 16,000,000 characters with `structure_too_large`, before any upload.
- **Breaking: `protein_find_similar` `databases`** accepts only `pdb100`, `afdb50`, `afdb-swissprot`, `afdb-proteome`, `cath50`, `bfvd_v3`, `mgnify_esm30`, `gmgcl_id`, and `bfmd`; any other name fails input validation ([#87](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/87)).
- **`protein_track_ligands` and the `protein_search_structures` `ligand` filter** count bound non-polymer copies only, so every component that also occurs as a polymer residue or glycan sugar now counts fewer entries ([#92](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/92)).
- **One 50 s upstream budget** covers each call of `protein_get_structure`, `protein_get_annotations`, `protein_find_similar`, and `af://`. A call it stops reports a timeout, except that under `predicted` and `best_available` an ID it stops becomes a `failed[]` row of `protein_get_structure`, and a `protein_find_similar` job Foldseek already accepted returns `status: "computing"` with its ticket.
- **A one-word `organism` value** matches an exact taxon name (scientific or NCBI common); a word fragment matches nothing ([#86](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/86)). When a one-word value matches nothing, the notice of `protein_search_structures` and `protein_analyze_collection` suggests the full species name or an NCBI taxon ID.
- **`method`** on `protein_search_structures` and `protein_analyze_collection` takes the 13 RCSB methods or an alias (`cryo-EM`, `X-ray`, `NMR`); any other value fails with `unknown_method` ([#86](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/86)).
- **Framework files** synced to `@cyanheads/mcp-ts-core` ^0.13.18, including the `add-skill` framework skill.

## Fixed

- **`protein_get_annotations` InterPro memberships** are read to completion (up to 5 pages of 200) and `domainsTotal` is InterPro's count; an empty answer from InterPro (HTTP 204) is no memberships, not an outage ([#81](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/81), [#83](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/83)).
- **Merged, demerged, and deleted UniProt accessions** are resolved the same way on every accession-keyed surface: a merged accession answers for its successor with a notice, a demerged or deleted one fails `inactive_accession` with its `replacements`, and a never-assigned one fails `accession_not_found` on `protein_get_annotations` and `af://` ([#83](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/83)).
- **`protein_find_similar` `by: "sequence"` with `pdb_id`** searches a protein entity, never a DNA or RNA chain ([#84](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/84)).
- **Entity order** is fixed by entity number for organisms, polymer entities, ligands, and binding sites, so identical calls return identical results ([#85](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/85)).
- **`organism` and `method` filters** match scientific names, NCBI common names, and taxon IDs, and the 13 RCSB methods with their common aliases ([#86](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/86)).
- **Foldseek hits** outside `pdb100` and AlphaFold DB carry no unusable `id`, and their `source` follows the database ([#87](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/87)).
- **`protein_get_structure` `best_available`** no longer labels non-PDB models as PDB entries; it picks by UniProt coverage, then resolution, else by confidence ([#90](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/90)).
- **`protein_get_structure` under `predicted` and `best_available`**: an ID whose upstream lookup fails becomes a `failed[]` row instead of failing the batch. When nothing resolves and no ID is known to lack a structure (every miss an upstream failure or a budget stop), the call fails with the new `upstream_unavailable` (ServiceUnavailable, retryable, with `data.failed`) rather than `all_failed`.
- **`include_coords` and `sections`** inline the same coordinate text on `content[]` and `structuredContent`, and two IDs that resolve to one model (an accession and its AlphaFold DB entry ID, or a merged accession and its successor) page one coordinate file ([#94](https://github.com/cyanheads/protein-mcp-server/issues/94)).
- **Caller text echoed in notices** (repeated IDs, unmatched `sections` entries, an `organism` value, a `find_ligand` query, a component ID) is Markdown-escaped, and `ligand` and `comp_id` are trimmed before matching.

## Security

- **Coordinate output is bounded**: `include_coords` and `sections` return coordinates in 24,000-character windows (or an outline when several selected files together exceed one window), at most 24,000 characters of file text are held while measuring, and `protein_find_similar` stops its coordinate download at 16,000,000 characters (`structure_too_large`).
